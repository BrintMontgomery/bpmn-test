# OFC-004 — Case Manager Intakes Consumer

## Actors

* Case Manager  
* Consumer  
* Social Worker  
* Nursing Staff  
* Floor Staff  
* Prospective Treatment Advocate  
* Prospective Contact

## Context

The Oklahoma Forensic Center requires a standardized case-management admission process to establish emergency and restricted contacts, document the consumer’s treatment advocate, verify authorized contacts, and ensure required release documentation is available to staff.

## Preconditions

* Admission notification email and associated consumer report have been received.  
* Consumer has completed the preceding security process and is available for admission activities.  
* Behavioral Health Case Manager has access to the Oklahoma State Courts Network (OSCN).  
* Elopement Form is available.  
* Admission packet is available; the packet component identified in this interview is the Treatment Advocate Form.  
* Release of Information (ROI) Form is available.  
* Unit-specific contact sheet is available through Floor Staff.  
* Consumer chart is available for filing completed ROI documentation.

## Outcome

The consumer’s elopement information, protective-order restrictions, treatment advocate status, and approved external contacts are documented and communicated to the appropriate staff. Required ROI documentation is placed in the chart, and any delayed or unresolved admission activity is tracked for follow-up and handoff to Social Worker or Floor Staff as appropriate.

Version

* Oklahoma Forensic Center — 2026-08-11 — v1.1

## Main Path

1. **Case Manager** receives the admission notification email and report and begins the Elopement Form.  
2. **Case Manager** reviews the admission report (for emergency contacts, county contacts, attorneys, sheriff information, victims, and other information needed for the Elopement Form.)  
3. **Case Manager** searches OSCN for protective orders and records applicable contact restrictions on the Elopement Form \[a\].  
4. **Case Manager** obtains remaining consumer information after arrival and completes the Elopement Form.  
5. **Case Manager** emails the completed Elopement Form to other admissions staff (after the consumer has arrived and the required information has been obtained.)  
6. **Case Manager** reviews the Treatment Advocate Form from the admission packet with the **Consumer**.  
7. **Consumer** identifies a prospective external Treatment Advocate.  
8. **Case Manager** contacts **Prospective Treatment Advocate** and obtains confirmation that the person is willing to serve in that role.  
9. **Prospective Treatment Advocate** signs the Treatment Advocate Form, and the **Consumer** signs the associated Release of Information authorization \[b\].  
10. **Case Manager** scans a copy of the completed treatment-advocate documentation for retention  
11. **Case Manager** mails a copy to the Treatment Advocate.  
12. **Nursing Staff** completes the nursing portion of the admission before the Case Manager performs the subsequent contact review.  
13. **Case Manager** checks with the **Social Worker** and **Consumer** for any additional persons to be added to the unit contact sheet.  
14. **Case Manager** confirms that the current unit contact sheet reflects approved contacts and communicates verified contact information to Floor Staff \[c\].  
15. **Case Manager** ensures each approved ROI generated through the admission contact process is placed in the consumer chart.  
16. **Case Manager** completes the case-management admission workflow and delivers ongoing contact, behavioral, or consumer-support issues to the **Social Worker** (or **Floor Staff**) as appropriate.

## Options from main path:

### Option A — Consumer Requests an Additional Contact

Trigger: The Consumer requests that another person be added to the unit contact sheet.

1. Case Manager completes an ROI with the Consumer.  
2. Case Manager records the authorized purpose or information categories on the ROI, including applicable contact purposes such as visit, telephone call, or televisit.  
3. Case Manager contacts the Prospective Contact and asks whether the person agrees to receive contact from the Consumer.  
4. If the Prospective Contact agrees, Case Manager informs Floor Staff that the contact information may be added to the unit contact sheet, and then Case Manager places the approved ROI in the consumer chart.  
5. If the Prospective Contact declines, Case Manager informs the Consumer and does not authorize the contact.

### Option B — Protective Order Restricts Contact

Trigger: OSCN identifies an active protective order involving a proposed or existing contact.

1. Case Manager records the restricted person on the Elopement Form.  
2. Case Manager does not authorize contact with the restricted person.  
3. Case Manager informs the Consumer of the restriction when the Consumer requests prohibited contact \[a\].

### Option C — Consumer Is Too Agitated or Delusional to Complete the Process

Trigger: The Consumer is too agitated, delusional, or otherwise unable to participate effectively in the case-management admission activities.

1. Case Manager postpones the affected interview or documentation activity.  
2. Case Manager returns when the Consumer is able to participate, including the following day when appropriate.  
3. Case Manager continues periodic follow-up during the week when needed to determine whether the Consumer can provide additional contact information or make decisions concerning contacts.

### Option D — Treatment Advocate Changes

Trigger: The Consumer requests a different Treatment Advocate after the initial designation.

1. Case Manager processes the new Treatment Advocate designation.  
2. Case Manager obtains the required consent and release documentation for the new Treatment Advocate.  
3. Case Manager updates the relevant records and distributes the resulting documentation according to the standard treatment-advocate process \[d\].

### Option E — Contact List Changes After Admission

Trigger: The Consumer requests that a person be added to or removed from the contact sheet after the initial admission process.

1. Case Manager processes the requested change.  
2. Case Manager completes and verifies an ROI when disclosure or external contact authorization is required.  
3. Case Manager communicates the approved change to Floor Staff \[c\].

### Option F — Case Manager Witnesses an Event Requiring Clinical Documentation

Trigger: The Case Manager personally witnesses an event that requires documentation in Avatar.

1. Case Manager documents the event in Avatar.  
2. Case Manager does not enter routine Elopement Forms, Treatment Advocate Forms, or admission ROIs into Avatar solely as part of this intake workflow \[e\].

## Notes

\[a\] Protective orders identified through OSCN restrict contact with the persons named in the order. A restricted person must not be approved for consumer contact.

\[b\] Release of information is limited to the information or purposes authorized on the signed ROI. Authorization is not a general release of all consumer information.

\[c\] The contact sheet is individualized to the unit and is not a standardized standalone case-management form. Floor Staff maintains the operational contact information.

\[d\] A Consumer may change the designated Treatment Advocate at any time and may request additions or removals from the contact sheet after admission.

\[e\] Avatar is not part of the routine case-management admission-document workflow described in this interview. The Case Manager uses Avatar when an observed event independently requires documentation.
